玩法說明

  1. 投幣（上方按鈕，或點機台右上的投幣口）換 20 顆彈珠，會從出珠口掉進下方托盤。
  2. 每局開始時，底部落獎區的燈會一直隨機閃。這時可以點藍色按鈕旁的「倍率孔」（白邊黑心的圓孔）：倍率分 2、4、6、8、10 倍，每升一段扣 2 顆，手上不足 2 顆就不能升。倍率越高，定燈後亮的燈越少（2 倍亮 5 顆，10 倍只亮 1 顆）。
  3. 按藍色按鈕定燈，決定這一局哪幾排有獎。定燈後就不能再投倍率孔。
  4. 定燈後點拉桿下方的「投珠孔」（白邊黑心的圓孔）投珠，投幾顆都可以，左上角數字顯示投了幾顆。按住投珠孔會自動連續投，按越久投越快。
  5. 在檯面上按住（滑鼠或手指）拉彈簧，放開發射，按越久彈越遠。
  6. 鋼珠落進有亮燈的那一排才算中，得珠 = 倍率 × 投珠數，從出珠口掉出來。
  7. 不知道下一步要做什麼時，看機台上閃爍黃框的地方。

彈珠台大冒險

  1. 按「冒險」（或 Q）切換到大冒險模式，隨時都能切回經典模式，兩邊的彈珠分開保留。
  2. 每場出現一隻像素怪物，上方的倍率欄變成怪物螢幕，顯示怪物的 HP、目前倍率和亮燈數。
  3. 每場有 30 顆彈珠，不能投幣。玩法和經典模式一樣，但中獎不出珠，改成對怪物造成「倍率 × 投珠數」點傷害。
  4. 把怪物 HP 打到 0 就勝利；彈珠用完怪物還活著就落敗。

How to play

  1. Insert a coin (the Coin button, or tap the coin slot at the top right of the machine) for 20 balls. They drop from the payout chute into the tray below.
  2. At the start of each round the prize lights at the bottom keep flashing at random. While they flash, tap the MULTIPLY hole next to the blue button to raise the multiplier: ×2, ×4, ×6, ×8, ×10. Each step costs 2 balls (you can't raise it with fewer than 2). The higher the multiplier, the fewer lights stay on after locking (×2 keeps 5, ×10 keeps just 1).
  3. Press the blue button to lock the lights. This decides which lanes pay out this round. After locking you can't raise the multiplier.
  4. Then tap the BET hole under the lever (the white ring with a black center) to bet as many balls as you like. The top-left counter shows your bet. Hold the BET hole to keep betting automatically, faster the longer you hold.
  5. Press and hold anywhere on the board (mouse or finger) to pull the spring, then let go to shoot. Hold longer to shoot further.
  6. The ball only wins if it lands in a lit lane. A win pays multiplier × bet, dropped from the payout chute.
  7. Not sure what to do next? Look for the flashing yellow frame on the machine.

Pinball Quest

  1. Press Quest (or Q) to switch to Pinball Quest. You can switch back to Classic at any time; each mode keeps its own balls.
  2. Each game a pixel monster appears. The multiplier columns at the top become a monster screen showing its HP, your multiplier and how many lights are lit.
  3. You get 30 balls per game and can't insert coins. Play works the same as Classic, but a win pays no balls: it deals multiplier × bet damage to the monster instead.
  4. Bring the monster's HP to 0 to win. Run out of balls while it's still alive and you lose.
