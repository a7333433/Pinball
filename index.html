玩法說明

  1. 投幣（上方按鈕，或點機台右上的投幣口）換 20 顆彈珠，會從出珠口掉進下方托盤。
  2. 每局開始時，底部落獎區的燈會一直隨機閃。這時可以點藍色按鈕旁的「強化孔」（白邊黑心的圓孔）：倍率分 2、4、6、8、10 倍，每升一段扣 2 顆，手上不足 2 顆就不能升。倍率越高，定燈後亮的燈越少（2 倍亮 5 顆，10 倍只亮 1 顆）。
  3. 按藍色按鈕定燈，決定這一局哪幾排有獎。定燈後就不能再投強化孔。
  4. 定燈後點拉桿下方的「投珠孔」（白邊黑心的圓孔）投珠，投幾顆都可以，左上角數字顯示投了幾顆。按住投珠孔會自動連續投，按越久投越快。
  5. 在檯面上按住（滑鼠或手指）拉彈簧，放開發射，按越久彈越遠。只是輕點一下不會發射，投珠時點偏投珠孔也不會不小心射出去。
  6. 鋼珠落進有亮燈的那一排才算中，得珠 = 倍率 × 投珠數，從出珠口掉出來。
  7. 不知道下一步要做什麼時，看機台上閃爍黃框的地方。

彈珠台大冒險

  1. 按「冒險」（或 Q）切換到大冒險模式，隨時都能切回經典模式，兩邊的彈珠分開保留。
  2. 開始前自己選要挑戰哪一隻怪物（星星越多越難，打倒過的會打勾）。上方的倍率欄變成怪物螢幕，怪物下方顯示牠的 HP；落獎區下方則變成訊息螢幕，顯示目前的狀況。
  3. 每場有 50 顆彈珠，不能投幣。每回合最多投 3 顆珠，過關獎勵「投珠」可以提高。大冒險打怪時倍率固定 ×1、亮 7 顆燈。強化孔最多投 3 顆，每投 1 顆，發射道最下面的金色強化魔法陣耐久 +1；經過它的珠變成強化珠（珠外多一圈轉動的金環），落進紅燈時傷害（扣完防禦後）×1.5、落進補珠燈時補珠數 ×1.5。這回合最後一顆經過的珠會吸走強化魔法陣剩下的所有耐久，每多吸 1 層倍率再 +0.5（2 層 ×2、3 層 ×2.5，珠外的金環也變成 2、3 圈），所以投珠比強化少時不會浪費；天賦魔法陣沒有這個效果。強化魔法陣不佔「一顆珠只吃一個魔法陣」的名額，所以強化珠也可以同時是燒紅珠或外傷珠（珠的顏色是天賦的顏色，外面再加金環）。投進強化孔的珠不會退回。火焰燈（最終傷害 ×1.5；紅燈都強化過再升第二階，暗紫色火焰、×2）只能靠雷射筆（紅）、高功率雷射筆（紫）做出來；用電路板或鋁箔包飲料把燈變成補珠燈時，火焰燈會變成綠色火焰的補珠燈，補珠數也 ×1.5（第二階 ×2）。投強化孔後手上至少要留 1 顆投珠，所以少於 2 顆時不能再投強化孔。玩法和經典模式一樣，但投幾顆珠，彈簧上就疊幾顆鋼珠，拉一次彈簧整疊一起射出，每顆各自結算；中獎不出珠，改成對怪物造成傷害。
  4. 每隻怪物都有防禦（顯示在 HP 旁邊的盾牌數字）：實際傷害 = 攻擊 − 防禦。打不破防禦就是 0 傷害，怪物還會竊笑。越後面的怪物 HP 和防禦越高，前期要打好幾回合才能打倒。
  5. 定燈後，沒亮燈的排裡會隨機多亮一顆綠色的補珠燈。鋼珠落進補珠燈不會造成傷害，改成每顆補 2 顆彈珠（強化珠 ×1.5）。
  6. 釘子隨關卡增加：第 1 關檯面上沒有釘子，之後每過一關都會長出新釘子，到最後一關巫妖時釘陣才完整。每一關的釘陣都是固定的，而且左右對稱。自由挑戰依怪物的關卡決定釘子。
  7. 彈力柱：左右牆邊各有 4 根紅白橡膠柱，貼著牆滑下來的彈珠會被彈回中間。
  8. 特殊釘子（打怪時才有，每關位置固定、左右成對，越後面越多）：燒紅釘——碰過的彈珠落進攻擊燈、造成傷害時，怪物燃燒 +1 層（任何天賦都會燃燒，跟燒紅彈珠疊在一起）；螺旋尖刺——彈珠會被彈開並分裂成 2 顆小珠，分裂後的彈珠不會再分裂；破洞——彈珠掉進洞口就直接消失，但吃了一顆珠後洞就被填住，這回合後面的珠會直接滾過去，下一回合才清空。點一下特殊釘子，訊息螢幕會顯示它的效果。
  9. 怪物技能（怪物螢幕名字下方顯示，點怪物螢幕看說明）：蝙蝠「吸血」這回合沒有珠造成傷害就回 5 血；蜘蛛「結網」每回合在 2 根釘子上結網；木乃伊「繃帶」每回合在兩根釘子間拉一條繃帶，最多纏住 3 顆珠（網、繃帶的第一顆碰到的珠被黏住，之後的珠會被它彈開，落珠結束後收走）；幽靈「穿透」攻擊燈的珠有 15% 被閃避；南瓜頭「南瓜燈」每回合隨機一排，進珠回 5% 血；骷髏「骨盾」可以抵擋 10 點彈珠傷害，每 2 回合恢復耐久；重力毒蠍（沙漠）「重力」這一關彈珠重量最重（跟「加重」拉滿一樣），撞到東西幾乎不彈；小惡龍「噴火」定燈後燒掉一顆攻擊燈（紅燈）；地獄犬「撲火」燃燒傷害減半；巫妖「魔法護盾」每一排擋下第一顆珠，每 3 回合補滿；殭屍「再生」每回合結束回 2% 血；吸血鬼「吸珠」每顆沒落進燈的珠讓牠回 2 血；石像怪「石化」血量越少越硬，剩 80%／50%／20% 以下時所有傷害減 20%／40%／60%。
  10. 半血狂暴：有技能的怪物 HP 掉到一半以下會狂暴（怪物發出紅光），技能變強，直到打倒為止：蜘蛛每回合 3 張網；幽靈閃避 25%；南瓜燈回 8% 血；殭屍每回合回 4%；木乃伊每回合 2 條繃帶；骷髏骨盾可抵擋 15 點傷害；吸血鬼每顆回 4 血；小惡龍紅燈、綠燈各燒掉 1 顆；地獄犬也會噴火燒掉 1 顆燈；巫妖每 2 回合補滿護盾。蝙蝠不會狂暴；石像怪本來就會隨血量減傷，也不會狂暴。
  11. 疊珠加成：同一回合有好幾顆鋼珠落進同一顆燈時，第 2 顆起每多一顆就多加 1：攻擊燈是攻擊 +1、+2、+3…（扣防禦前），補珠燈是多補 1、2、3… 顆。
  12. 把怪物 HP 打到 0 就勝利；彈珠用完怪物還活著就落敗。
  13. 卡片上方可以切換「冒險模式」（預設）和「自由挑戰」。冒險模式從城堡出發，經過草原、山洞、墓園、沙漠、地城、火山、魔王城共 14 關，第 1 關固定是史萊姆，第 14 關固定是魔王巫妖。每次冒險的路線都不一樣：出發時先在城堡選第 1 關的兩個節點之一，之後每過一關就在地圖上選下一站（每層 2～3 個節點，只能走連得到的路；節點只看得出是魔物、菁英、寶箱、事件、商人還是魔王，看不出是哪一隻），節點有遭遇魔物（照場景出現對應的怪物）、菁英魔物（HP ×1.33、防禦 +1，打倒後可以選 2 次過關獎勵）、寶箱和隨機事件（勇者祭壇、神秘賭徒、受傷的冒險者、詛咒祭壇、迷路，看描述選擇要怎麼做，可能花珠換獎勵或道具，也可能失去彈珠；事件中得到的彈珠會等下一關開始才從出珠口掉出來）、商人（隨機擺出 3 樣道具，一般 50%、稀有 30%、史詩 15%、傳說 5%，價錢 5／10／20／50 顆彈珠；買走的就沒了，可以花彈珠重骰 3 格，每次 +50%；付完至少要留 1 顆）。打魔王前一關一定有商人。怪物的 HP、防禦照第幾關決定，不管遇到哪一隻。一開始 50 顆彈珠，剩下的彈珠會帶到下一關，每過一關再補珠（打倒魔物、打開寶箱、走完事件、逛完商人都算；15 顆起，隨等級增加），失敗就要從第 1 關重來。螢幕右上角會顯示目前第幾關。
  14. 天賦：冒險模式開始前先選 1 個天賦，效果持續整趟冒險（自由挑戰沒有天賦）。按天賦旁邊的「說明」可以看效果和專屬過關獎勵。
    • 合金鋼珠：每顆珠攻擊 +1（扣防禦前）、暴擊率 +15%。
    • 魔法陣：畫在發射道上，往上經過的珠會變成對應的特殊珠（換顏色）。每經過一顆珠耐久 −1，用完就碎掉，每回合開始回滿；一顆珠只會吃一個魔法陣。被搖晃機台直接放到檯面上的珠不會經過發射道。
    • 燒紅彈珠：發射道上有 1 個燒紅魔法陣（耐久 3）。燒紅珠落進攻擊燈讓怪物燃燒 +1 層（打不破防禦也算）；每回合結束受到「層數」點無視防禦的傷害，之後層數減半，換怪物時歸零。
    • 外傷滾珠：每回合紅燈少 2 顆、補珠燈多 2 顆，發射道上有 1 個外傷魔法陣（耐久 3），外傷珠落進補珠燈時照常補珠，另外造成等於補珠數的無視防禦傷害。
    每個天賦另有 4 個專屬過關獎勵（卡片有橘框），只有選了該天賦才抽得到：合金鋼珠有破甲（攻擊時無視 1 點防禦）、鎢鋼（獨特的，暴擊率 +10%；暴擊時完全無視防禦）、連鎖（暴擊率 +10%；同一回合內每暴擊一次，後續的彈珠攻擊 +1）、鍍層（獨特的，彈珠攻擊 +1，彈珠不會掉入破洞）；燒紅彈珠有助燃（燃燒 +1）、悶燒（獨特的，回合結束時減少的燃燒減少 50%）、爆燃（暴擊率 +10%；暴擊時燃燒 +1）、灼熱（獨特的，暴擊率 +10%；燃燒傷害也會暴擊）；外傷滾珠有撕裂（外傷 +25%）、碎甲（每回合 1 次，外傷時怪物防禦 −1）、重創（獨特的，暴擊率 +10%；外傷傷害也會暴擊，外傷暴擊倍率 200%）、放血（暴擊率 +10%；外傷暴擊時補珠 +1，要先有重創）。
  15. 闖關紀錄：選天賦的畫面下面有「闖關紀錄」按鈕，可以看每個天賦的挑戰次數、最遠打到第幾隻、最少回合通關、通關最多剩珠、單回合最大傷害與補珠、單顆彈珠最大傷害與補珠，以及最近 10 次冒險的回合數、最後持有彈珠、總共花費彈珠、單回合最大傷害、單回合最大補珠、單顆彈珠最大傷害、單顆彈珠最大補珠。
  16. 等級：從 Lv1 開始，每過一關升 1 級（打倒魔物、打開寶箱、走完事件都算，所以走寶箱、事件不會比一路打怪弱）。每升一級，之後的過關補珠多 2 顆（第 1 關後補 15、第 2 關後 17…）；每 2 級每顆珠攻擊 +1（扣防禦前）。自由挑戰會當作前面的怪物都打過，等級照算。目前等級顯示在螢幕右上角和狀態面板。

寶箱、裝備與道具

  1. 地圖上的寶箱關卡：寶箱 HP 1，落獎區的燈全亮白燈。定燈前每投 1 顆強化珠（最多 3 顆），寶箱就升一級：1 顆變 3 排藍燈，2 顆再多 2 排紫燈，3 顆再多 1 排橘燈。按藍色按鈕定燈後投 1 顆珠發射，落在哪一排，就從那個顏色等級的裝備裡抽 3 件讓你選 1 件：白燈一般、藍燈稀有、紫燈史詩、橘燈傳說（已經有的裝備不會再抽到）。
  2. 裝備放在裝備欄就整趟冒險都有效，不用使用；裝備欄最多 3 格，滿了再拿新的要選一件換掉，或放棄新的。背包的「裝備」頁可以看目前的裝備。
    一般：好運骰子吊飾（每次選過關獎勵多 1 次免費重骰）、好運彈珠袋（每回合最後一顆珠帶紅色星光，落進攻擊燈必定暴擊）、好運護身符（寶箱稀有燈 +2、一般燈 −2）
    稀有：子母彈珠袋（每回合最後一顆珠離開發射道時分裂成兩顆，還能再被螺旋尖刺分裂）、玩具魔法棒（每回合定燈後一顆攻擊燈變成魔法燈，落進的珠隨機觸發火焰／外傷／強化魔法）、疊疊樂積木吊飾（每次疊珠算 2 次）
    史詩：紅色棒棒糖、綠色棒棒糖（每回合多一個耐久 3 的火焰／外傷魔法陣，已經有就耐久 +2）、彈跳彈珠袋（沒落進燈的珠會往回彈 1 次）
    傳說：連點滑鼠（傳說以外的道具觸發 2 次）、強化護符（怪物關卡每回合強化孔一開始就 3 層）、龍珠吊飾（每花 1 顆珠造成魔物上限 HP 1% 傷害，每 7 回合再造成 7%）
  3. 道具要按「使用」才有效果，從商人買，或從隨機事件「受傷的冒險者」拿到，放在托盤右邊的背包按鈕（或 I）裡，只能在冒險模式的怪物關卡使用。每個道具有使用時機：定燈前、定燈後（發射前），或兩者都可。
  4. 闖關失敗、從第 1 關重來時，背包和裝備都會清空。

過關獎勵與暴擊

  1. 冒險模式每打倒一隻怪物（最後一隻除外），會從獎勵池隨機抽 3 樣讓你選 1 樣，效果持續整個冒險、可以疊加。不滿意可以重骰：每次先送 1 次免費重骰（裝備「好運骰子吊飾」再多 1 次），用完才花彈珠：第 1 次的費用等於剛打完的關卡數（打完第 3 關就是 3 顆），之後每次再多 50%（無條件進位）。每張卡片會顯示「目前 → 拿了之後」的數值，也可以按「查看數值」看全部數值。
  2. 獎勵包含：強化（彈珠攻擊 +1）、倍化（彈珠基礎攻擊 +100%）、紅燈（每回合攻擊燈 +1）、穿透（破防時 +1 真實傷害）、加重（減少彈跳幅度 50%）、補珠強化（補珠量 +1，最多兩次）、綠燈（每回合補珠燈 +1）、暴擊率（+20%）、暴擊傷害（倍率 +50%）、投珠（發射上限 +2）。寫著「獨特的」的獎勵只能拿一次。
  3. 命中紅燈時有機會暴擊：基礎暴擊機率 5%、暴擊傷害 150%，可以靠過關獎勵提升。
  4. 新的冒險開始時，過關獎勵會全部清空。
  5. 冒險模式的進度會自動存在瀏覽器：玩到一半關掉頁面，下次打開可以選「繼續冒險」接著玩，或「重新開始」。回合打到一半關掉時，還沒發射的珠會退回，已經在檯面上的珠算用掉。

How to play

  1. Insert a coin (the Coin button, or tap the coin slot at the top right of the machine) for 20 balls. They drop from the payout chute into the tray below.
  2. At the start of each round the prize lights at the bottom keep flashing at random. While they flash, tap the BOOST hole next to the blue button to raise the multiplier: ×2, ×4, ×6, ×8, ×10. Each step costs 2 balls (you can't raise it with fewer than 2). The higher the multiplier, the fewer lights stay on after locking (×2 keeps 5, ×10 keeps just 1).
  3. Press the blue button to lock the lights. This decides which lanes pay out this round. After locking you can't raise the multiplier.
  4. Then tap the BET hole under the lever (the white ring with a black center) to bet as many balls as you like. The top-left counter shows your bet. Hold the BET hole to keep betting automatically, faster the longer you hold.
  5. Press and hold anywhere on the board (mouse or finger) to pull the spring, then let go to shoot. Hold longer to shoot further. A quick tap does not shoot, so missing the BET hole will not fire your balls by accident.
  6. The ball only wins if it lands in a lit lane. A win pays multiplier × bet, dropped from the payout chute.
  7. Not sure what to do next? Look for the flashing yellow frame on the machine.

Pinball Quest

  1. Press Quest (or Q) to switch to Pinball Quest. You can switch back to Classic at any time; each mode keeps its own balls.
  2. Before each game, pick which monster to fight (more stars = harder; ones you've beaten get a check mark). The multiplier columns at the top become a monster screen, with its HP shown below it, and the area under the prize lanes becomes a message screen.
  3. You get 50 balls per game and can't insert coins. You can bet at most 3 balls per round; the More Balls reward raises this. When fighting in Quest the multiplier is always ×1 with 7 lights lit. You can put up to 3 balls in the boost hole; each one adds 1 durability to the gold boost circle at the bottom of the launch lane. Balls that pass through it become boosted balls (a spinning gold ring around the ball): ×1.5 damage on red lights (after DEF) and ×1.5 balls on refill lights. The last ball through it each round soaks up all the durability left, +0.5 per extra layer (2 layers ×2, 3 layers ×2.5, with 2 or 3 gold rings), so betting fewer balls than your boost wastes nothing. Talent circles don't do this. The boost circle doesn't count toward the one-circle-per-ball limit, so a boosted ball can also be red-hot or grinding (the ball takes the talent color, with the gold ring on top). Balls put in the boost hole are not refunded. Flame lights (final damage ×1.5; once every red light is a flame light, one upgrades again to a dark purple flame for ×2) now only come from the Laser Pointer (red) and High-power Laser (purple). If a Circuit Board or Juice Box turns lights into refill lights, flame lights become green-flame refill lights that pay ×1.5 balls (×2 when upgraded twice). You must keep at least 1 ball to bet, so you can't use the boost hole with fewer than 2 balls. Play works the same as Classic, but every ball you bet stacks on the spring, and one pull shoots them all, and each ball scores on its own. A win pays no balls: it damages the monster instead.
  4. Every monster has defense (the shield number next to its HP): damage = attack − DEF. If your attack doesn't beat its defense it deals 0 and the monster snickers. Later monsters have more HP and defense, so early fights take several rounds.
  5. After locking, one unlit lane also gets a green refill light. Landing there deals no damage; each ball gets 2 balls back (×1.5 for a boosted ball).
  6. Pins grow each stage: stage 1 has no pins at all, and every stage after that adds new pins until the full field returns at the last stage (the Lich). Each stage's layout is fixed and mirrored left to right. Free fight uses the pins for that monster's stage.
  7. Rubber posts: four red-and-white posts on each side wall knock balls sliding down the wall back toward the middle.
  8. Special pins (monster stages only, fixed mirrored spots, more in later stages): Red-hot pin — a ball that touched it adds 1 burn when it deals damage on a red light (any talent can burn; stacks with Red-hot Marble); Spiral spike — knocks the ball away and splits it into two smaller balls (split balls can't split again); Hole — a ball that drops into it simply vanishes, but then the hole is plugged by that ball and the rest of the round rolls over it; it is cleared next round. Tap a special pin to see what it does on the message screen.
  9. Monster skills (shown under the name on the monster screen; tap the screen for details): Bat heals 5 HP if no ball deals damage in a round; Spider webs 2 pins and Mummy ties a bandage between two pins each round that holds up to 3 balls (for a web, the first ball to touch it sticks, later balls bounce off it, and it is taken when the round ends); Ghost dodges 15% of attack-light balls; Pumpkin Head turns a random lane into a pumpkin lamp each round that heals 5% HP per ball; Skeleton has a bone shield that absorbs 10 ball damage and is restored every 2 rounds; the Gravity Scorpion (desert) makes marbles as heavy as possible (like a maxed Weight reward), so they barely bounce; Baby Dragon burns one attack light (red) after you lock; Hellhound takes half burn damage; Lich wards every lane against the first ball, refilled every 3 rounds; Zombie heals 2% HP every round; Vampire heals 2 HP per ball that lands on no light; Gargoyle gets harder as it loses HP: below 80%/50%/20% HP all damage is cut by 20%/40%/60%.
  10. Rage at half HP: a monster with a skill that drops to half HP or less becomes enraged (it glows red) and its skill gets stronger until it is beaten: Spider webs 3 pins; Ghost dodges 25%; the pumpkin lamp heals 8%; Zombie heals 4% per round; Mummy ties 2 bandages; Skeleton's bone shield absorbs 15 damage; Vampire heals 4 HP per ball; Baby Dragon burns 1 red and 1 green light; Hellhound also burns 1 light; Lich refills its wards every 2 rounds. The Bat never rages, and neither does the Gargoyle, which already hardens as it loses HP.
  11. Stacking bonus: when several balls land on the same light in one round, each one after the first adds 1 more: +1, +2, +3… attack on a red light (before DEF), or +1, +2, +3… extra balls on a refill light.
  12. Bring the monster's HP to 0 to win. Run out of balls while it's still alive and you lose.
  13. Switch between Adventure (the default) and Free fight at the top of the card. Adventure sets out from the castle through the grassland, cave, graveyard, desert, dungeon, volcano and demon castle: 14 stages, always starting with the Slime and ending with the Lich. Every run has a new route: at the castle you pick one of two first-stage nodes, then after each stage you pick the next stop on the map (2–3 nodes per stage, following the paths; nodes only show monster, elite, treasure, event, merchant or boss, not which monster). Nodes are monsters (matching the area), elites (HP ×1.33, DEF +1; beating one lets you pick 2 stage rewards), treasure chests and random events (Heroes' Altar, Mysterious Gambler, Wounded Adventurer, Cursed Altar, Lost: read the story and choose; you may trade balls for rewards or items, or lose some balls; balls you gain during an event drop out when the next stage starts) and merchants (3 random items: Common 50%, Rare 30%, Epic 15%, Legendary 5%, costing 5 / 10 / 20 / 50 balls; bought items are gone, you can pay to reroll all 3 slots, +50% each time, and you must keep at least 1 ball). The stage before the boss always has a merchant. Monster HP and DEF depend on the stage, not on which monster you meet. You start with 50 balls, keep whatever is left, get bonus balls after every stage you clear (monster, chest, event or merchant; 15 and up, growing with your level), and losing sends you back to stage 1. The current stage shows at the top right of the screen.
  14. Talents: before an Adventure, pick 1 talent that lasts the whole run (Free fight has none). Tap Info next to a talent to see its effect and exclusive rewards.
    • Alloy Marble: +1 attack per ball (before DEF), +15% crit chance.
    • Magic circles sit on the launch lane; a ball passing up through one turns into that kind of special ball (it changes color). Each ball uses up 1 durability, the circle shatters when it runs out, and it refills at the start of every round. A ball takes only one circle. Balls dropped straight onto the table by Shake the Machine skip the lane.
    • Red-hot Marble: one red-hot magic circle (3 uses) on the launch lane. Each red-hot ball on a red light adds 1 burn (even if DEF blocks it); at the end of every round the monster takes damage equal to its burn, ignoring DEF, then burn halves. Burn resets for each monster.
    • Grinding Marble: 2 fewer red lights and 2 more refill lights every round. One grinding magic circle (3 uses) on the launch lane; a grinding ball on a refill light still pays out, and also deals damage equal to the balls refilled (ignores DEF).
    Each talent also has 4 exclusive rewards (orange cards) that only show up with that talent. Alloy: Breach (attacks ignore 1 DEF), Tungsten (unique; +10% crit chance, crits ignore DEF completely), Chain (+10% crit chance; each crit this round gives the later balls +1 attack), Plating (unique; ball attack +1, balls no longer fall into holes). Red-hot: Kindle (burn +1), Smolder (unique; burn lost at round end −50%), Flare (+10% crit; crits add +1 burn), Searing (unique; +10% crit; burn damage can crit). Grinding: Rend (grind +25%), Shatter (once per round, grind lowers DEF by 1), Deep Wound (unique; +10% crit; grind can crit with a 200% multiplier), Bloodletting (+10% crit; grind crits refill +1 ball; needs Deep Wound).
  15. Run records: tap "Run records" under the talents to see, for each talent, your number of runs, furthest stage, fewest rounds for a clear, most balls left after a clear, best single-round damage and refill, best single-ball damage and refill, plus your last 10 runs (rounds used, balls left, balls spent, best round damage and refill, best single-ball damage and refill).
  16. Levels: you start at Lv1 and gain a level for every stage you clear (monster, chest or event alike, so taking chests and events never leaves you weaker). Each level adds 2 balls to later stage bonuses (15 after stage 1, 17 after stage 2…), and every 2 levels give +1 attack per ball (before DEF). Free fight counts the earlier monsters as defeated. Your level is shown top-right on the screen and in the stats panel.

Treasure chests, gear and items

  1. Treasure chests appear as nodes on the Adventure map. It has 1 HP and every prize light is lit white. Before locking, each ball in the boost hole (up to 3) upgrades the chest: 1 ball turns 3 lanes blue, 2 balls add 2 purple lanes, 3 balls add 1 orange lane. Lock, bet 1 ball and shoot: the lane you land in decides the tier, and you pick 1 of 3 random pieces of gear from that tier (never one you already have). White is Common, blue Rare, purple Epic, orange Legendary.
  2. Gear works for the whole adventure while it is equipped; you never use it. You have 3 gear slots: when they are full, a new piece replaces one of your choice, or you can leave it. The backpack's Gear page shows what you have.
    Common: Lucky Dice Charm (1 extra free reward reroll), Lucky Marble Pouch (the last ball of every round has red sparkles and always crits on an attack light), Lucky Amulet (chest rare lights +2, common −2)
    Rare: Mother-and-Child Pouch (the last ball of every round splits in two as it leaves the launch lane; spikes can still split them), Toy Magic Wand (after locking, one attack light becomes a magic light that triggers random Fire / Grind / Boost magic), Stacking Blocks Charm (every stack counts twice)
    Epic: Red / Green Lollipop (an extra fire / grinding magic circle with durability 3 every round, or +2 durability if you already have one), Bouncy Marble Pouch (a ball that lands on no light bounces back up once)
    Legendary: Auto-clicker Mouse (non-legendary items trigger twice), Boost Amulet (in monster stages the boost hole starts every round at 3 layers), Dragon Ball Charm (every ball you spend deals 1% of the monster's max HP, and every 7 rounds it deals 7%)
  3. Items must be used to take effect. Buy them from merchants or get them from the Wounded Adventurer event; they go into the backpack button right of the tray (or I) and can only be used in Adventure monster stages. Each item says when it can be used: before locking, after locking (before shooting), or either.
  4. Failing the campaign and starting over from stage 1 empties the backpack and your gear.

Stage rewards and crits

  1. In Adventure, every monster you beat (except the last) offers 3 random rewards; pick 1. Rewards last for the whole adventure and stack. You get 1 free reroll each time (the Lucky Dice Charm gear adds 1 more); after that, rerolls cost balls: the first costs as many balls as the stage you just cleared (3 after stage 3), and each one after costs 50% more (rounded up). Each card shows your current value and the value after picking it, and "View stats" opens the full stats panel.
  2. Rewards: Power (ball attack +1), Double (ball base attack +100%), Red Light (+1 attack light every round), Pierce (+1 true damage when you break DEF), Weight (bounces 50% less), Refill+ (refill amount +1, twice at most), Green Light (+1 refill light every round), Crit Rate (+20%), Crit Damage (multiplier +50%), More Balls (shot limit +2). Rewards marked Unique can only be taken once.
  3. Hits on red lights can crit: base crit chance 5% and crit damage 150%, raised by rewards.
  4. Rewards reset when a new adventure starts.
  5. Adventure progress is saved in your browser automatically. Close the page mid-run and next time you can pick Continue or Start over. Closing mid-round refunds balls not yet fired; balls already on the board are spent.
