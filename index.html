玩法說明

  1. 投幣（上方按鈕，或點機台右上的投幣口）換 20 顆彈珠，會從出珠口掉進下方托盤。
  2. 每局開始時，底部落獎區的燈會一直隨機閃。這時可以點藍色按鈕旁的「倍率孔」（白邊黑心的圓孔）：倍率分 2、4、6、8、10 倍，每升一段扣 2 顆，手上不足 2 顆就不能升。倍率越高，定燈後亮的燈越少（2 倍亮 5 顆，10 倍只亮 1 顆）。
  3. 按藍色按鈕定燈，決定這一局哪幾排有獎。定燈後就不能再投倍率孔。
  4. 定燈後點拉桿下方的「投珠孔」（白邊黑心的圓孔）投珠，投幾顆都可以，左上角數字顯示投了幾顆。按住投珠孔會自動連續投，按越久投越快。
  5. 在檯面上按住（滑鼠或手指）拉彈簧，放開發射，按越久彈越遠。
  6. 鋼珠落進有亮燈的那一排才算中，得珠 = 倍率 × 投珠數，從出珠口掉出來。
  7. 不知道下一步要做什麼時，看機台上閃爍黃框的地方。

彈珠台大冒險

  1. 按「冒險」（或 Q）切換到大冒險模式，隨時都能切回經典模式，兩邊的彈珠分開保留。
  2. 開始前自己選要挑戰哪一隻怪物（星星越多越難，打倒過的會打勾）。上方的倍率欄變成怪物螢幕，怪物下方顯示牠的 HP；落獎區下方則變成訊息螢幕，顯示目前的狀況。
  3. 每場有 50 顆彈珠，不能投幣。每回合最多投 3 顆珠，過關獎勵「投珠上限」可以提高。大冒險的倍率從 ×1 起跳、亮 7 顆燈，倍率孔每投 1 顆就加 1 倍、少一顆燈（最高 ×7 亮 1 顆）；加倍後手上至少要留 1 顆投珠，所以少於 2 顆時不能再投倍率孔。玩法和經典模式一樣，但投幾顆珠，彈簧上就疊幾顆鋼珠，拉一次彈簧整疊一起射出，每顆各自結算；中獎不出珠，改成對怪物造成傷害。
  4. 每隻怪物都有防禦（顯示在 HP 旁邊的盾牌數字）：實際傷害 = 倍率 − 防禦。打不破防禦就是 0 傷害，怪物還會竊笑。越後面的怪物 HP 和防禦越高，前期要打好幾回合才能打倒。
  5. 定燈後，沒亮燈的排裡會隨機多亮一顆綠色的補珠燈。鋼珠落進補珠燈不會造成傷害，改成每顆補 2 顆彈珠；這回合第一顆落進補珠燈時，還會退回投進倍率孔的珠。
  6. 釘子隨關卡增加：第 1 關檯面上沒有釘子，之後每過一關都會在隨機位置長出新釘子，到最後一關巫妖時釘陣才完整。自由挑戰依怪物的關卡決定釘子數。
  7. 疊珠加成：同一回合有好幾顆鋼珠落進同一顆燈時，第 2 顆起每多一顆就多加 1：攻擊燈是攻擊 +1、+2、+3…（扣防禦前），補珠燈是多補 1、2、3… 顆。
  8. 把怪物 HP 打到 0 就勝利；彈珠用完怪物還活著就落敗。
  9. 卡片上方可以切換「冒險模式」（預設）和「自由挑戰」。冒險模式要從第 1 關史萊姆一路打到第 14 關巫妖：一開始 50 顆彈珠，打倒怪物後剩下的彈珠會帶到下一關，每過一關再補珠（15 顆起，隨等級增加），失敗就要從第 1 關重來。螢幕右上角會顯示目前第幾關。
  10. 天賦：冒險模式開始前先選 1 個天賦，效果持續整趟冒險（自由挑戰沒有天賦）。
    • 合金鋼珠：每顆珠攻擊 +1（扣防禦前）、暴擊率 +15%。
    • 燒紅彈珠：每次落進攻擊燈讓怪物燃燒 +1 層（打不破防禦也算）；每回合結束受到「層數」點無視防禦的傷害，之後層數減半，換怪物時歸零。
    • 外傷滾珠：落進補珠燈時照常補珠，另外造成等於補珠數的無視防禦傷害。
    每個天賦另有 3 個專屬過關獎勵（卡片有橘框），只有選了該天賦才抽得到：合金鋼珠有破甲合金（無視 1 點防禦）、鎢鋼彈芯（暴擊完全無視防禦，只能拿一次）、連鎖暴擊（同一回合內每暴擊一次，這回合後面的珠攻擊 +1，下回合歸零）；燒紅彈珠有助燃（每次命中多疊 1 層）、悶燒（燃燒後保留 2/3、3/4…）、火種（補珠燈也疊 1 層）；外傷滾珠有撕裂（外傷比例 +25%）、碎甲（外傷觸發時怪物防禦 −1，每回合一次）、滾動補給（沒中燈的珠退回 1 顆，只能拿一次）。
  11. 等級：從 Lv1 開始，每打倒一隻怪物升 1 級。每升一級，之後的過關補珠多 2 顆（第 1 關後補 15、第 2 關後 17…）；每 2 級每顆珠攻擊 +1（扣防禦前）。自由挑戰會當作前面的怪物都打過，等級照算。目前等級顯示在螢幕右上角和狀態面板。

寶箱與道具

  1. 冒險模式每兩隻怪物之間有一個寶箱關卡：寶箱 HP 1，落獎區的燈全亮白燈。定燈前每投 1 顆倍率珠（最多 3 顆），寶箱就升一級：1 顆變 3 排藍燈，2 顆再多 2 排紫燈，3 顆再多 1 排橘燈。按藍色按鈕定燈後投 1 顆珠發射，落在哪一排就拿那個顏色等級的道具：白燈一般、藍燈稀有、紫燈史詩、橘燈傳說。
  2. 道具放在托盤右邊的背包按鈕（或 I）裡，只能在冒險模式的怪物關卡使用。每個道具有使用時機：定燈前、定燈後（發射前），或兩者都可。
  3. 闖關失敗、從第 1 關重來時，背包會清空。

過關獎勵與暴擊

  1. 冒險模式每打倒一隻怪物（最後一隻除外），會從獎勵池隨機抽 3 樣讓你選 1 樣，效果持續整個冒險、可以疊加。不滿意可以花彈珠重骰：第 1 次 1 顆，之後每次多 1 顆。
  2. 獎勵包含：投珠上限 +2、彈珠 +10、每珠增傷、基礎倍率 +1、攻擊燈數 +1（只能拿一次）、穿透傷害、加重彈珠、拔 5 根釘子、補珠加量、補珠燈數（只能拿一次）、寶箱升級、暴擊率、暴擊傷害。
  3. 命中紅燈時有機會暴擊：基礎暴擊機率 5%、暴擊傷害 150%，可以靠過關獎勵提升。
  4. 新的冒險開始時，過關獎勵會全部清空。
  5. 冒險模式的進度會自動存在瀏覽器：玩到一半關掉頁面，下次打開可以選「繼續冒險」接著玩，或「重新開始」。回合打到一半關掉時，還沒發射的珠會退回，已經在檯面上的珠算用掉。

How to play

  1. Insert a coin (the Coin button, or tap the coin slot at the top right of the machine) for 20 balls. They drop from the payout chute into the tray below.
  2. At the start of each round the prize lights at the bottom keep flashing at random. While they flash, tap the MULTIPLY hole next to the blue button to raise the multiplier: ×2, ×4, ×6, ×8, ×10. Each step costs 2 balls (you can't raise it with fewer than 2). The higher the multiplier, the fewer lights stay on after locking (×2 keeps 5, ×10 keeps just 1).
  3. Press the blue button to lock the lights. This decides which lanes pay out this round. After locking you can't raise the multiplier.
  4. Then tap the BET hole under the lever (the white ring with a black center) to bet as many balls as you like. The top-left counter shows your bet. Hold the BET hole to keep betting automatically, faster the longer you hold.
  5. Press and hold anywhere on the board (mouse or finger) to pull the spring, then let go to shoot. Hold longer to shoot further.
  6. The ball only wins if it lands in a lit lane. A win pays multiplier × bet, dropped from the payout chute.
  7. Not sure what to do next? Look for the flashing yellow frame on the machine.

Pinball Quest

  1. Press Quest (or Q) to switch to Pinball Quest. You can switch back to Classic at any time; each mode keeps its own balls.
  2. Before each game, pick which monster to fight (more stars = harder; ones you've beaten get a check mark). The multiplier columns at the top become a monster screen, with its HP shown below it, and the area under the prize lanes becomes a message screen.
  3. You get 50 balls per game and can't insert coins. You can bet at most 3 balls per round; the Bet Limit reward raises this. In Quest the multiplier starts at ×1 with 7 lights lit; each ball in the multiplier hole adds ×1 and turns off one light (up to ×7 with 1 light). You must keep at least 1 ball to bet, so you can't raise the multiplier with fewer than 2 balls. Play works the same as Classic, but every ball you bet stacks on the spring, and one pull shoots them all, and each ball scores on its own. A win pays no balls: it damages the monster instead.
  4. Every monster has defense (the shield number next to its HP): damage = multiplier − DEF. If your attack doesn't beat its defense it deals 0 and the monster snickers. Later monsters have more HP and defense, so early fights take several rounds.
  5. After locking, one unlit lane also gets a green refill light. Landing there deals no damage; each ball gets 2 balls back, and the first one this round also refunds the balls you put into the multiplier.
  6. Pins grow each stage: stage 1 has no pins at all, and every stage after that adds new pins in random spots until the full field returns at the last stage (the Lich). Free fight uses the pin count for that monster's stage.
  7. Stacking bonus: when several balls land on the same light in one round, each one after the first adds 1 more: +1, +2, +3… attack on a red light (before DEF), or +1, +2, +3… extra balls on a refill light.
  8. Bring the monster's HP to 0 to win. Run out of balls while it's still alive and you lose.
  9. Switch between Adventure (the default) and Free fight at the top of the card. Adventure runs from stage 1 (Slime) to stage 14 (Lich): you start with 50 balls, keep whatever is left after each win plus bonus balls per stage (15 and up, growing with your level), and losing sends you back to stage 1. The current stage shows at the top right of the screen.
  10. Talents: before an Adventure, pick 1 talent that lasts the whole run (Free fight has none).
    • Alloy Marble: +1 attack per ball (before DEF), +15% crit chance.
    • Red-hot Marble: each red-light hit adds 1 burn (even if DEF blocks it); at the end of every round the monster takes damage equal to its burn, ignoring DEF, then burn halves. Burn resets for each monster.
    • Grinding Marble: refill lights still pay out, and also deal damage equal to the balls refilled (ignores DEF).
    Each talent also has 3 exclusive rewards (orange cards) that only show up with that talent. Alloy: Armor Breaker (ignore 1 DEF), Tungsten Core (crits ignore DEF completely; once only), Chain Crit (each crit gives the later balls this round +1 attack; resets next round). Red-hot: Kindling (+1 burn per hit), Smolder (burn keeps ⅔, ¾… instead of half), Tinder (refill lights add 1 burn). Grinding: Rend (+25% grind damage), Shatter (grind lowers DEF by 1, once per round), Rolling Supply (balls that miss every light come back; once only).
  11. Levels: you start at Lv1 and gain a level for every monster you defeat. Each level adds 2 balls to later stage bonuses (15 after stage 1, 17 after stage 2…), and every 2 levels give +1 attack per ball (before DEF). Free fight counts the earlier monsters as defeated. Your level is shown top-right on the screen and in the stats panel.

Treasure chests and items

  1. In Adventure there is a treasure chest between every two monsters. It has 1 HP and every prize light is lit white. Before locking, each ball in the multiplier hole (up to 3) upgrades the chest: 1 ball turns 3 lanes blue, 2 balls add 2 purple lanes, 3 balls add 1 orange lane. Lock, bet 1 ball and shoot: the lane you land in decides the item's tier. White is Common, blue Rare, purple Epic, orange Legendary.
  2. Items go into the backpack button right of the tray (or I) and can only be used in Adventure monster stages. Each item says when it can be used: before locking, after locking (before shooting), or either.
  3. Failing the campaign and starting over from stage 1 empties the backpack.

Stage rewards and crits

  1. In Adventure, every monster you beat (except the last) offers 3 random rewards; pick 1. Rewards last for the whole adventure and stack. You can reroll for balls: 1 the first time, then 1 more each time.
  2. Rewards: +2 bet limit, +10 balls, per-ball damage, +1 base multiplier, +1 red light (once only), piercing damage, heavier marble, 5 fewer pins, bigger refills, more refill lights (once only), better chests, crit rate, crit damage.
  3. Hits on red lights can crit: base crit chance 5% and crit damage 150%, raised by rewards.
  4. Rewards reset when a new adventure starts.
  5. Adventure progress is saved in your browser automatically. Close the page mid-run and next time you can pick Continue or Start over. Closing mid-round refunds balls not yet fired; balls already on the board are spent.
